## Source-only reparse boundary and next fixture

[MS-FSA sharing](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/windows_protocols/ms-fsa/8c0e3f4f-0729-49f4-a14d-7f7add593819) excludes FILE_WRITE_ATTRIBUTES-only from its read/write/delete sharing gate. [SET_REPARSE_POINT](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/openspecs/windows_protocols/ms-fsa/4aeefef8-92c3-4abc-af7a-a610caf8a165) accepts FILE_WRITE_DATA or FILE_WRITE_ATTRIBUTES and rejects nonempty directories. Consequently strict deny-write sharing alone does not establish empty-directory in-place reparse prevention. Nonempty regular files have tag-specific restrictions: mountpoint requires a directory; symlink rejects nonzero stream size. Neither fact proves arbitrary-tag immutability for executable/source/log files. These are specification deductions, not successful native attacks.

Anchored nonempty mutable-output directories and already-held child entries may support a bounded policy, but empty immutable runtime directories and exact inventory/bootstrap consumers require an explicit reviewed contract. No silent anchors, inventory exclusions, ACL/trust/OS policy changes or blanket shareWRITE have been introduced.
