**Method**
- Each comic was opened read-only (zip archive opened for reading, first three and last two pages copied to scratch) and compared with its GCD row: cover title, issue or volume number, publisher logo and cover date, file name and folder. The publication line (indicia) was read for 6 rows (T1, T10, 71, 76, 92, 125). Credits were compared by eye only for T1 and T10. Year and Writer were not checked row by row.
- comics.org answered one headless request with a Cloudflare block page, so it was not retried. Every verdict is from the comic itself and the row fields.
- Rates are per rule, from the random sample only; the 11 targeted rows are not random and are not in the rates. Wrong per 1,000 = wrong rows / rows sampled x 1,000. The whole-list estimate weights each rule by its size in the full list (12,689). "Upper" is the exact one-sided 95% bound (Clopper-Pearson). With 25 to 45 rows per rule and 0 or 1 wrong, the per-rule bounds are wide; only the overall figure is tight enough to lean on.

**Patterns**
1. **A file name that is not a plain issue number slips past the single-issue / collection split.** `003 (2025))`, `(Complete Vol)` and `[1 of 3]` all became "unnumbered" and matched a one-issue or collected record. In the full list the three American Splendor: Unsung Hero `[1 of 3]`, `[2 of 3]`, `[3 of 3]` files share one collected-edition record (likely wrong, not opened). The 52 rows in Ender's targeted list share the T2 risk.
2. **Language is not in the name.** One untagged Russian edition in 200 rows (5 of 5 in its folder). The planned tag fix will not catch it; the cover is the only clue.
3. **"Only English issue with that series and number" is not proof.** X-Files Special Edition looked unique because the Topps record is filed under another name and only a Trielle Komix record matched.
4. **Marvel Legends reissues** (above): a policy call for Sergei before the Star Wars Legends tree is written.
5. **The new "same-name series one year off" skip-guard rejects good matches.** All 6 targeted rows of that kind (T3, T4, T5, T6, T7, T10) are right; the Savage Sword of Conan folder is genuinely the Marvel UK weekly. Skipping is safe, but it costs 52 correct rows.
6. **The "never replace a cover-read Publisher" ruling would keep a wrong value in T1** (DC Comics on a Dark Horse book). All 19 replacement rows are right matches: 12 Marvel -> Marvel UK (correct, UK weekly), 6 First Comics -> Devil's Due / 1First Comics (matches the cover logo), 1 DC Comics -> Dark Horse Comics (correct).

**Side findings from the full CSV (not wrong matches, but fields that could hurt)**
- GTIN: 2,402 of 4,678 GTIN writes (51%) carry a code shared by two or more different GCD issues (493 codes). It is the series-level 12-digit UPC without the issue add-on (for example Catwoman #64, #66 and #79 all get 761941229065). If the app treats GTIN as an identity key this will collide.
- Count: of 1,019 files with `(of N)` in the name, 108 get a Count different from N (10.6%). Most are series that have a #0 or several arcs (Cross 6 vs 7, Star Wars: Dark Times 5 vs 17, Clone Wars 6 vs 12), so GCD's number may be the better one, but it is a policy call.
- 439 GCD issues are claimed by two or more files (928 rows, 7.3%). Most have the same parsed number (variant covers, rescans, a cover sketch file); 7 issues are claimed by files with different parsed numbers.

**Holds before writing starts (these make the GO)**

2. Hold the Star Wars Legends tree (217 rows) until Sergei says whether a Marvel Legends reissue takes the Dark Horse original's data.
3. Do not write GTIN when the code is shared by more than one GCD issue.

**Not checked:** Year and Writer row by row; the indicia of most rows; any row outside this sample.

## How to check a row

1. Find the comic under `Y:\Comix\` (path in the table). Open it in YACReader to view only; do not save or edit it.
2. Open the GCD link. Compare the cover, the title, the issue number or volume number, and the cover date or year with the comic.
3. Write a verdict in the last column:
   - **R:** right issue or edition. A variant cover of the right issue counts as R.
   - **W:** a wrong issue, volume, edition (single issue vs collection, US vs UK) or language.
   - **?:** cannot tell.
4. For a W, add a few words on what it should be.
5. If time allows, glance at Year and Writer against the comic's credits page.
